# From… to?

The lowest counts of those forced to leave their homes.
From death, through death, hoping to reach life.

A side view of migration routes, 2014–2024, drawn in p5.js as watercolour on paper.
Every person in the record is one dot (at sea) or one brush line (on land).
They leave from one side; where they died or went missing, they go down.
The dead settle and build the floor; the missing stop on the way and leave a faint stain; survivors keep going.
Night and day pass over the page: at night only the boats' lights, and on land the walkers' small lights, stay visible.

- **Sea / Land buttons:** preset routes by direction (North, South, East, West, Centre).
- **From … To …:** every route in the data.
- **Time / Wetness sliders:** speed of the years, and how wet the paper gets.

## Files
- `index.html` – page and controls
- `sketch.js` – the p5.js sketch (parameters at the top)
- `routes.json` – the data, prepared from the source below

## Data
IOM Missing Migrants Project, incidents from 2 January 2014 to 31 December 2024 (19,632 records with coordinates).
Figures are minimums: only deaths and disappearances that were recorded.
The place of each death along a route is an approximation, projected from the incident's coordinates onto the route's main axis.
